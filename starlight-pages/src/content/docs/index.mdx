## More Than An Inventory

Some guitars arrive as long-held dreams. Others are inexpensive, borrowed, rescued, rebuilt, or simply impossible to forget. This archive keeps their specifications and histories together, alongside amps, future pedalboards, practice notes, and the projects still taking shape.
