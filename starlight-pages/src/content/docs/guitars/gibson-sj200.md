## The Story

This guitar began as a used, non-electrified instrument. Before delivery, TFOA installed an [LR Baggs HiFi Duet](https://www.tfoa.eu/products/lr-baggs-hifi-duet) and performed a professional PLEK service. That installation is part of this guitar's story, not an original Gibson factory feature.

The dealer described it as powerful and open, with clarity, shimmering highs, and enough dynamic range for strumming, flatpicking, or accompanying a voice. Its listing noted light play wear where the arm rests on the top, while the rest of the instrument remained very clean.

## Installed Electronics

The HiFi Duet combines bridge-plate sensors with a microphone and onboard blending controls. It was selected as a minimally invasive way to add an amplified voice without pretending the guitar left Bozeman with factory electronics.
