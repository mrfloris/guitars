## Why The 1960 Reissue Is Different

1960 was the final year of the original Les Paul Standard run. Gibson's reissue keeps the classic construction while changing the feel and a few visible details: a slimmer neck, reflector volume and tone knobs, and double-ring tuner tips. This is the version for someone who wants vintage construction without the fuller 1958-style neck.
