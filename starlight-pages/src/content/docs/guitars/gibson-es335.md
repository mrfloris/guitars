## The Story

This guitar followed a long search for a left-handed ES-335 that truly spoke to me. It was made to specification for a dealer in 2024, and Gibson later confirmed the model, build year, Made 2 Measure origin, and detailed build record.

It is the sort of guitar that makes a collection feel personal rather than complete. I bought myself a little bit of happiness, and it was worth the wait.
