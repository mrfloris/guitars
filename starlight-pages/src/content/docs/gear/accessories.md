## Hardshell Case Storage

The first dedicated case-storage purchase is a [String Swing CC29 Guitar Case Floor Rack](https://www.thomann.nl/string_swing_cc29_guitar_case_floor_rack_bw.htm). It was chosen to begin organising multiple hardshell cases, including wider Gibson cases, without stacking them against walls or furniture.

The rack still needs a real-world review: stability with mixed case sizes, fit for the SJ-200 and ES-335 cases, floor depth, padding, and whether another rack is eventually needed for guitars that live outside their cases.

## Safety Before Style

Gibson Custom instruments use sensitive nitrocellulose finishes. Any stand, hanger, padding, display cabinet, or cleaning product needs a material check before it touches a bare guitar for long periods. Case-storage capacity should also be judged by loaded weight and case width, not only by the advertised number of slots.
