The wishlist is deliberately a research list, not a shopping cart. Prices, availability, exact configurations, and private buy notes stay in the working archive until an idea becomes a real project.

## Guitars

### Furch Built-To-Order Left-Handed Acoustic

The main future guitar idea is a unique [Furch](https://furchguitars.com/en/) build from Czechia. Dreadnought-cutaway and grand-auditorium-cutaway directions are being compared, along with an optional boost/electronics choice. The unfinished configuration codes remain private until the body shape and specification are settled.

## Amps And Digital Rigs

- [Circa '74 AV150-10 Amp in Mahogany](https://www.tfoa.eu/products/circa-74-av150-10-amp-mahogany): compare its intended use, output, speaker, room fit, and playing feel with repairing the current Marshall.
- [NUX MG-50Li](https://nuxaudio.com/product/mg50li/): compare an integrated modelling and effects route with the planned individual-pedal board.
- A quiet headphone or studio-monitor option for practice and experimentation.

## Pedalboard

- Board, case, isolated power supply, patch cables, and reliable mounting hardware.
- Wah, compressor, overdrive, RAT-style distortion, reverb, chorus, phaser, and flanger.
- Utility pedals and enough routing flexibility to explore a smaller parallel board later.

## Room And Display

- Additional hardshell-case storage if the first String Swing rack works well.
- A matching guitar floor stand for instruments kept outside their cases.
- [Sonicely Bluesbreaker-style display panels](https://www.tfoa.eu/products/sonicely-m-style-bluesbreaker-levant-dark-blue-gold-piping).
- An enclosed [Silverclouds LED showcase](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1182676176/electric-guitar-showcase-cabinet-for), only after nitro safety and mounting strength are confirmed.
- Custom 3D-printed guitar accessories and room decorations.

## Buying Rule

Every item needs a clear job, safe materials, realistic room dimensions, and compatibility with the equipment already here. A good demo link is useful; a confirmed return policy and a quiet signal chain are better.
