# Publication Policy

The public Starlight site is a curated layer built from the private research archive under `resources/`. Private source material is never copied wholesale into the public site.

## Public By Default

- Confirmed guitar and equipment model names.
- Manufacturer specifications and public product codes.
- Year, finish, handedness, construction, electronics, and hardware details.
- Broad first-person stories approved for the collection archive.
- Public manufacturer, dealer, reference, tab, lesson, and product links.
- General project status such as owned, formerly owned, on loan, build project, or wishlist.
- Maintenance and modification summaries that do not expose private records.
- Public-safe personal photos after serial numbers, addresses, labels, and reflections have been checked.

## Review Before Publishing

- Purchase year, seller, condition, repair history, and modification history.
- Exact personal stories involving family, friends, neighbours, relationships, finances, or insurance.
- Wishlist configurations, shopping links, prices, and availability.
- Photos or text originating from dealers, manufacturers, emails, or other third parties.
- Details about borrowed equipment or its owner.
- Any fact that is based only on memory or conflicts with another source.

## Keep Private

- Guitar serial and lot numbers.
- Invoice, order, payment, shipment, and Gmail identifiers.
- Exact purchase prices, VAT details, payment dates, receipts, and invoices.
- Names, direct phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses, and private correspondence.
- Insurance providers, coverage amounts, appraisals, claims, and proof-of-value records.
- Raw email exports, downloaded documents, private bookmarks, and source archives.
- Dealer photos unless reuse rights are confirmed.
- Location or security details that could put the collection or its owner at risk.

## Accuracy Rules

- Label recollections and unresolved identifications instead of presenting them as confirmed facts.
- Prefer the specific instrument's records over a similar or current product page.
- Explain when an official reference page describes a different model year.
- Paraphrase source descriptions and link to the source rather than copying sales text.
- Keep private provenance intact even when only a sanitized summary is published.
